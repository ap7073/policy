Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 June 2026

Travixo ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the Travixo mobile application.

By using Travixo, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Travixo allows users to generate ride invoices and trip bills. Information may include:

  • Customer Name
  • Mobile Number (Optional)
  • Pickup Location
  • Destination
  • Trip Date
  • Distance
  • Fare Amount
  • Additional Notes

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Passwords
  • Bank Account Details
  • Debit/Credit Card Information
  • Aadhaar Number
  • PAN Card Information
  • Contacts
  • SMS Messages
  • Call History
  • Biometric Information

3. PDF Bill Generation

Travixo generates PDF invoices directly on your device. Your trip information is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Device Storage

The application may request storage permission to:

  • Save generated PDF bills
  • Share invoices
  • Export ride receipts

Travixo does not access your personal files unrelated to the application.

5. Location Information

Travixo does not automatically access your GPS location. Pickup and destination information are entered manually by the user.

6. Advertising

Travixo may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may collect anonymous information such as:

  • Advertising ID

  • App Usage
  • Crash Reports

We use commercially reasonable methods to protect your information. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure.
